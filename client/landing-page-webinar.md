## DESIGN SYSTEM NOTES FOR THE DEVELOPER

**Palette.**

- Primary background: warm ivory, around #FBF6EC
- Secondary background: pale parchment, around #F0E9DA
- Primary text: deep ink black, around #1A1A1A
- Body text: warm charcoal, around #2C2A26
- Accent: burnt sienna or deep saffron, around #B85C2E
- Source wash and dividers: soft warm gray, around #C7BFB1

**Type.** A serif for headlines and pull quotes, paired with a humanist sans-serif for body and UI. The serif should feel editorial and confident, with hints of Indian editorial design tradition. Recommended pairings: Tiempos Headline or Source Serif Pro for headlines, paired with Inter or IBM Plex Sans for everything else. Avoid Garamond and Times.

**Imagery.** Warm, desaturated, with the burnt sienna accent preserved in each photo. Favor architectural details from modern Indian university campuses, light through windows in serious academic spaces, hands writing, books on a table, an empty seminar room. The page should feel like the visitor has stepped into the office of a thoughtful vice-chancellor, not a marketing funnel.

**Spacing.** Editorial proportions. 96 to 120 pixels of vertical padding between sections on desktop. Generous line height in body copy. The page is unhurried.

**Animation.** Minimal. A subtle fade-in on scroll for the evidence strip and the quote cards. Nothing more.

**Mobile.** Every section stacks to a single column. Evidence strip stacks vertically with the same editorial treatment. Type scales down by 20%. CTA in hero remains above the fold on every device.

**Registration form.** Modal or separate page, identical in style to the landing page. Six fields maximum: name, title, institution, institutional email, optional submitted question, optional checkbox for continuing education certificate.

**Performance.** Target sub-1.5-second first contentful paint. Optimize images to WebP. No carousels. No video.

**The principle.** This page persuades through evidence, editorial discipline, and ambition. The institutions that read it should feel that the host understands their market, respects their time, and is offering a conversation that will sharpen the strategic decisions they are already trying to make. The restraint is the credibility.

## DESIGN SYSTEM NOTES FOR THE DEVELOPER

**Type.** One serif for headlines, one sans-serif for everything else. Recommended pairing: a high-contrast modern serif at display sizes (something in the family of Söhne Breit, GT Sectra, or Tiempos Headline), paired with Inter or Söhne for body and UI. No more than two type families on the entire page.

**Color.** Black, white, and a single accent color used only on the buttons. Recommended accent: a deep gold or warm amber, drawn from the brand palette, used sparingly. Every other surface is black or white. No gradients. No drop shadows. No gray boxes containing content.

**Spacing.** Each section should hold one idea. Use 120 to 180 pixels of vertical padding above and below each section on desktop. The page should feel like it has too much white space, not too little. If a section feels crowded, the answer is more space, not less content.

**Animation.** Subtle. A fade-in on scroll for the numbers in section two. A slight upward translation on the headline in the hero, completing in under 400ms. Nothing else. The page should not perform.

**Mobile.** Every section collapses to a single column. Type sizes scale down by roughly 30%. The four numbers in section two stack vertically with the same fade-in behavior. The CTA button in the hero remains above the fold on every device width.

**Registration form.** Lives on a separate page or in a modal triggered by the CTA. The landing page itself never shows form fields. The button takes the visitor to a clean form: institution name, role, email, optional submitted question. Five fields maximum. The form converts because the page has already done the persuasion.

**Loading and performance.** The page is mostly type. There is no excuse for it to be heavy. Target a sub-1-second first contentful paint. No carousel libraries. No video backgrounds. No third-party scripts beyond the registration handler and analytics.

**The principle.** Every design choice on the page should be defensible against the question: does this serve the registration? If the answer is no, it comes off the page. The visitor's attention is the most valuable thing on the screen. Spend it carefully.

## A FINAL NOTE ON THE BUILD

The hero and the closing are the two sections that do the conversion work. Everything between them is the proof. If the page were cut down to only sections one, three, and ten, it would still convert at a meaningful rate. The middle sections exist to give the visitor enough confidence to register, and no more than that. Resist the urge to add another section, another testimonial, another row of logos, another paragraph of context. The discipline of the page is what makes it persuasive. The restraint is the design.

Build it black, white, serif, and quiet. Let the words carry the weight. The visitor will register because the page took her seriously enough to say one thing well, and trusted her to know what to do with it.
